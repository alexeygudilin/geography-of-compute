# Geography of Compute

A research demonstration by Nelvarx Mathematical Laboratory, Nelvarx Systems AB.

This repository contains two complementary research reports:

- **Structural Analysis:** System architecture, dependencies, constraints, and operational risks.
- **Mathematical Modelling:** Feasibility, reachability, stability, and scenario-based calculations.

The research uses hypothetical configurations to explore AI data center siting potential.

Mathematical modelling does not constitute proof of real-world site suitability.

The internal Nelvarx methodology remains proprietary.
